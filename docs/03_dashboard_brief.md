## 1. Business Objective
This report is designed to provide visibility about catalog's distribution, price health and product availability for digital catalog, allowing decision-making based on a clean and structured dataset.

## 2. Target Audience
Commercial Manager / E-Commerce Category Manager: Responsible for product catalog margin, product rotation and customer experience in digital platforms.

## 3. Business Questions
1. How are products distributed among the different main categories?
2. What's the price catalog behavior and how is it related with product ratings?
3. Which percentage of the catalog is available for online sales?

## 4. KPIs
Total Unique Products: Total volume of active products in IKEA Mexico subset.
Median Price of the Catalog: Helps understand base price positioning.
Average Global Rating Score: Customer perception of the products.
Online Availability Rate: Percentage of the catalog available for e-commerce.

## 5. Visual Architecture and Design
Horizontal Bar Graph:  Distribution of product catalog between main categories.
Scatterplot: Relation between product price and ratings to identify outliers and best products.
Dynamic Filters (Slicers): main category and online availability segmentation.
